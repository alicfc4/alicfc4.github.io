Research

My work connects laser acoustics, two-dimensional materials, thermoelastic composites, and multiphysics modelling.

01

Photoacoustics & Laser Ultrasonics

Generation and characterization of broadband acoustic waves using pulsed optical excitation.

02

Graphene & 2D Materials

Low-dimensional materials for photothermal conversion and advanced optoacoustic emitters.

03

Piezophotonic & Thermoelastic Composites

Nanomaterial–polymer architectures for efficient optical-to-acoustic conversion.

04

Ultrafast Laser–Matter Interaction

Fast energy deposition and coupled optical, thermal, and mechanical processes.

05

Wave Propagation & NDT

Acoustic-wave propagation and non-destructive testing applications.

06

Computational Modelling

Multiphysics simulation and scientific computing using COMSOL, MATLAB, and Python.
