Research

My work focuses on laser-generated ultrasound and photoacoustic emitters based on two-dimensional materials and functional composites, supported by experimental characterization and multiphysics modelling.

Research themes

01Acoustic generation

Laser Ultrasonics & Photoacoustics

2D Materials & Functional Composites

04Numerical analysis

Multiphysics Modelling

Computational analysis of coupled optical, thermal, mechanical, and acoustic phenomena.

Current Project

From optical excitation to acoustic emission

The current PhD project studies how ultrathin and low-dimensional absorbers can be integrated with polymer matrices to improve laser-driven acoustic generation while balancing optical absorption, thermal transport, and thermoelastic response.
