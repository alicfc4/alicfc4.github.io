Profile

Academic profile

My current research focuses on low-dimensional nanomaterials, laser ultrasonics, photoacoustics, and nanomaterial-based piezophotonic composites. I work across both experimental and computational research, with an emphasis on scientific modelling, data analysis, and optical and acoustic measurement techniques.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a Master's degree in Optics–Laser Physics at the University of Isfahan, where my research focused on electron transport in two-dimensional systems with tilted Dirac cones.
