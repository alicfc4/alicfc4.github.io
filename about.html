Profile

Academic profile

My research focuses on laser-generated ultrasound and photoacoustic generation using two-dimensional materials and thermoelastic composites. I combine experimental work with numerical modelling to study optical absorption, heat transfer, thermoelastic response, and acoustic generation.

My current work includes graphene- and MoS₂-based composite emitters, optical and acoustic measurements, scientific data analysis, and coupled thermal–mechanical–acoustic simulations.
