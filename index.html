About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Ljubljana and a member of the LASTEH (Laboratory for Laser Techniques) research group. My research focuses on low-dimensional-based nanomaterials, laser ultrasonics, photoacoustics, and nanomaterial-based piezophotonic composites.

I have experience in both experimental and computational research, scientific publishing, and interdisciplinary collaboration, with strong skills in numerical modeling, scientific programming, and optical measurement techniques.

Research Interests

01

Photoacoustics & Laser Ultrasonics

Generation, propagation, and characterization of broadband acoustic waves using pulsed optical excitation.

Graphene & 2D Materials

Low-dimensional materials for photothermal conversion and advanced optoacoustic emitters.

03

Piezophotonic & Thermoelastic Composites

Nanomaterial–polymer composite architectures for efficient optical-to-acoustic conversion.

04

Ultrafast Laser–Matter Interaction
