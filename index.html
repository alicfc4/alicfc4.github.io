About Me

My research focuses on the interaction of laser light with advanced materials for the generation of high-frequency acoustic waves. I am particularly interested in photoacoustic and optoacoustic conversion using two-dimensional materials and polymer-based composite structures.

Research Interests

01

Laser-Generated Ultrasound

Broadband acoustic-wave generation through pulsed optical excitation.

02

Photoacoustics

Optical-to-thermal-to-acoustic energy conversion in thin-film emitters.

03

2D Materials

Graphene, MoS₂, and related materials for advanced photothermal systems.

04

Functional Composites

Polymer-based composite structures for efficient optoacoustic generation.
